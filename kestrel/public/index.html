      How orders are placed

      How Kestrel keeps you safe

      • It only uses the budget you give it, and never touches anything else in your account.
      • Every position has a trailing stop that only moves up.
      • A bad day (past the daily limit) stops new buys until midnight UTC.
      • A deep drop from the peak (past the safety limit) sells everything and pauses until you resume.
      • It stops itself after 5 failed orders in a row, or an unusual number of orders in a day.
      • It skips orders when a price looks wrong (more than 15% from the last close).
      • Real money is switched on here only, never by voice. Voice orders with real money need a tap on screen.
      • The dashboard only answers on this computer, and every request needs this session's secret token.

      No trading system can promise profits. Prices can fall fast, and past results don't guarantee future ones. Only trade money you can afford to lose.
