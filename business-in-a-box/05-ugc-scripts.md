# 05 · UGC video scripts

Both scripts are written for TikTok, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels at 9:16 (1080 × 1920), with captions burned in. The presenter speaks **as Marquee**, never as a venue owner or customer. If an AI avatar is used, add "AI presenter" to the caption.

Delivery: warm, low-key, slightly gravelly, about 155 words per minute, eye contact with the lens, no "hey guys".

---

## Script A: "The Receipt" (about 35 s)

**Setting:** a presenter at a bar top in an empty venue in the afternoon, with house lights low and a printed receipt in hand (or a receipt graphic overlaid).

| Time | Voiceover | On-screen text / visual |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | "Last Friday, your fans paid three forty-four a ticket in fees. Your venue kept zero." | **$3.44 → them / $0 → you** (red, then the $0 drops in) |
| 3–9 s | "That's the ticket platform's cut. Every ticket, every show, all year." | Receipt graphic, fee lines struck through in red |
| 9–19 s | "Marquee gives you your own box office: on your website, paid into your own Stripe. You build a two-dollar booking fee into one honest price, and after card fees you keep about a dollar seven." | **$27 all-in → you keep $1.07** (green) |
| 19–27 s | "Three hundred tickets a week? That's about sixteen and a half grand a year. Flat two forty-nine a month. No cut from us." | **~$16.6k / year** · **$249/mo flat** |
| 27–35 s | "Want your number? Tap below and we'll run your last month of shows." | CTA: **See what you'd keep →** · end card "OWN THE DOOR" |

Fine-print caption: *Based on $25 tickets, 2 per order, a $2 venue fee and Stripe's 2.9% + 30¢. Your numbers will differ.*

---

## Script B: "Doors at 7" (about 40 s)

**Setting:** handheld point-of-view walk through an empty room at 3 PM: chairs up, stage, bar, front door. Cut to a door person scanning a phone ticket. The presenter appears from 12 s.

| Time | Voiceover | On-screen text / visual |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | "If you run a room that holds three hundred people, this one's for you." | **300-cap room?** over the empty stage |
| 3–12 s | "Right now, a ticket site sits between you and every fan. It takes a fee on each ticket, owns the checkout, and makes your fans pay more." | Slow push on the front door · **fee on every ticket** |
| 12–24 s | "We build you your own box office in seven days. Your logo, your domain, your Stripe account. Door staff scan tickets on their phones. Your list is yours." | Presenter on camera · quick cuts: branded checkout, phone scan, customer export |
| 24–32 s | "Fans see one all-in price, usually lower than they pay now, and the booking fee stays with the house." | **Fans pay less. The house keeps the fee.** |
| 32–40 s | "Marquee. Own the door. Book a ten-minute fee check: link below." | End card letterboard "OWN THE DOOR" + CTA **Book a fee check →** |

---

## Production options

1. **Real presenter (best):** the founder films both scripts in a real venue on a weekday afternoon. That takes about an hour with a phone and a lavalier mic.
2. **AI avatar:** needs an avatar video tool. Graph, as used in the reference video, isn't connected to this workspace. Paste the scripts in once a tool is connected and keep the "AI presenter" label.
3. **Faceless edit:** voiceover generated with the connected ElevenLabs account, laid over captions and venue b-roll (licensed stock or footage you shoot).
