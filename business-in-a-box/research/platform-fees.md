# Platform fees: other ticketing platforms indie venues use (US)

**Checked 2026-11-20.** Figures come only from each platform's own pages (pricing pages, help centres, published terms). Third-party comparison sites were read but never used as a source.

**Benchmark.** Doorkeep on a $25 ticket: the fan pays $27.75 all-in, which is a $2.75 booking fee ($2.00 to the venue plus 75¢ to Doorkeep). After Stripe the venue keeps about $1.05 a ticket. Doorkeep has no monthly fee.

**Pitchable test.** A platform is pitchable only if the buyer pays **more than $2.75** in fees on a $25 ticket **and** no venue share is published. "Venue share" here means any published way for the venue to receive part of what buyers pay in fees. That covers a split or rebate of the platform's fee, and a fee the venue sets, adds to or bundles into the buyer fees, and keeps.

**How the $25 figures are worked out.** Percentages are applied to the $25 face value. Fixed card fees are counted once, as for a one-ticket order. Card processing is included only where the platform publishes the rate and lets it be passed to the buyer. "Max" means the organiser passes every fee on to the buyer. Where no amount is published, the cell says unknown. Nothing is estimated.

## Summary

**Pitchable: none of the 14.** No platform clears both tests. The two that charge buyers more than $2.75 both publish a way for the venue to share in the fee.

**Not pitchable (7)**, with the buyer fee on a $25 ticket:
- SimpleTix: max $2.32 ($1.29 platform fee plus processing), and it allows a venue custom fee.
- ThunderTix: $2.10 (Pass-Through) or $2.20 (Pass-Through Plus).
- TicketSource: $1.87.
- Crowdwork: $2.03.
- Ticket Tailor: max $1.88 if every fee is passed on, otherwise $0. It also allows a venue booking fee.
- Prekindle: $3.09 with turnkey processing, $2.34 with the venue's own Stripe. It fails only because it publishes a venue rebate.
- DICE: $3.25 (13%, US self sign-up). It fails only because it publishes a fee split with organisers. Venues on the self sign-up terms get no share, but you can't tell which terms a venue has from outside.

**Unknown (7):** Turntable Tickets (flat fee not published), Showpass (CAD only), TuneHatch, VBO Tickets, Opendate, SeatEngine and Tixr. Turntable and Opendate also publish venue-set fees, so they would fail the venue-share test even if their fee were high.

**What this means for the pitch.** "Fans pay less and the venue gains about $1.05" fits Eventbrite-style platforms, where the fan pays more than $2.75 and the venue keeps nothing. Most indie platforms here either charge fans less than $2.75 or let the venue take a cut. Prekindle and DICE are the nearest fits. Before pitching one of their venues, check its checkout. On Prekindle, any fee above the standard $1.59 + 3% (+3% turnkey) means the venue is already adding a rebate.

## Reliability notes

- **ThunderTix:** search snippets disagreed with each other. One showed a "CA$0.76 per seat" setup fee, an older ThunderTix white paper lists the GA plan at $1.00/ticket, and a third-party blog was titled "ThunderTix Fees in 2027". Only the live pricing page (modified 2026-10-16) was used.
- **TicketSource:** the page prints "0.99¢". Its own calculator shows a $1.34 fee on a $10 ticket (3.5% + $0.99), so it means $0.99.
- **Showpass:** the pricing page says prices are displayed in CAD. No USD schedule was found, so there is no USD figure.
- **Turntable:** the only fee statement found is a blog post from May 2025.
- **How the pages were read:** most pages were read with Firecrawl's AI page query. Showpass and TicketSource were also checked against the raw page text.
- **Card fees:** the 30¢ card fee is counted once per order. On multi-ticket orders the per-ticket fee is slightly lower.
