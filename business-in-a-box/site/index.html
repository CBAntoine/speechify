Straight answers

Won't we lose the ticket site's discovery?

Most of your buyers already come from your own Instagram, your mailing list and your posters. You can keep a couple of discovery listings on the big sites and move your regulars to your own box office.

Is adding our own booking fee allowed?

Yes, as long as it's part of the price fans see first. The FTC's fee rule (in force since May 12, 2025) requires all-in pricing for live-event tickets, and we set your box office up that way.

Who handles refunds and chargebacks?

You do, in your own Stripe dashboard, the same place your money lands. We'll walk you through it at kickoff.

What about door sales and reserved seats?

Keep your bar POS for walk-ups. If you need reserved seating or a door box office, those need a paid licence from Hi.Events, which we pass through at cost.

What if something breaks on a Friday?

Your box office runs on its own server, with monitoring and nightly backups, and there's a Friday-night response plan. Check-in works from any phone browser.

Do you take a percentage?

No. We charge a flat 75¢ per paid ticket (50¢ under $20), built into your all-in price. There's no monthly fee and no setup fee.
